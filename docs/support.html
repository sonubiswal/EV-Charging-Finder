Common Issues

  • Charging station not connecting to the app
  • Vehicle not charging properly
  • Error messages on the screen

FAQs

How do I install the EV Charging System?

Follow the installation guide provided in the package. Ensure a certified electrician handles the wiring and setup for safety and compliance.

Why is my vehicle not charging?

Ensure the charging cable is securely connected to both the vehicle and the station. Check for any error messages and consult the manual.

How can I update the software?

Connect the charging system to your Wi-Fi network and navigate to the settings menu in the companion app to check for updates.
